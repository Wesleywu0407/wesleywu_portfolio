ORIGINAL MODEL SOURCE

Xbot.glb from the official three.js example assets:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/examples/models/gltf/Xbot.glb

The GLB in this package preserves the original mesh, skeleton and animation clips.
The mercury/Y2K look is applied separately at runtime by the included Three.js
material helper.

Before publicly redistributing the model, review the current licensing and asset
terms in the original three.js repository.
